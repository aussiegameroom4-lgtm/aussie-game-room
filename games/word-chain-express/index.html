    How to Play

    SUN➞LIGHT➞HOUSE➞BOAT

    SUN + LIGHT = SUNLIGHT · LIGHT + HOUSE = LIGHTHOUSE · HOUSE + BOAT = HOUSEBOAT

    • 🔗Build the chain. Tap a word that joins onto the last word to make one real word. Keep going until the chain is complete.
    • ⚠️Watch for dead ends. Some words make a real word but lead nowhere. They break your chain.
    • 🔥Streaks. Right answers in a row boost your points, up to 4x.
    • 💡Stuck? Tap Hint and a right word will glow. In the Campaign a hint means that carriage can earn 2 stars at most. In other modes it costs points (or 5 seconds in Blitz).
    • 📖Word Book. Every word you make is saved in your Word Book. Can you collect them all?
    • 🌟Secret words. Two leftover words that join each other are worth +1,000. Your first tap on a secret word is a free hint.

    Game modes

    • 🎖️Campaign. Mission 1: work your way up the train, one carriage at a time. You get 3 hearts per carriage, and the hearts you keep become stars. 👹 Each boss carriage has its own trick: misspelt words, lights out, or leaking steam!
      Mission 2 unlocks when you reach the driver: make the longest chain you can, but you're never told how long it can go.
      Mission 3, Points & Junctions, unlocks after Mission 2: switch the points on a rail map so the train's route links into real words, then depart. 3 departures per map, and every map is new.
      Mission 4, The Missing Coupling, unlocks after Mission 3: find the word that joins two carriages (SUN ▢ HOUSE = LIGHT) against the clock. Later levels make you type the answer!
      Mission 5, The Midnight Mystery, is the final mission and unlocks after Mission 4: search 10 carriages for clues and catch the diamond thief among 12 suspects. The thief is different every time!
    • 📅Daily Challenge. One new puzzle a day, the same for everyone. One attempt only, so keep your streak alive!
    • ♾️Endless. 3 lives. The chains keep getting longer.
    • ⚡Blitz. 60 seconds. +3s for a right word, −5s for a wrong one, +5s for every chain.
    • 🎫Passenger Class. Every point you earn moves you up seven classes, from Economy all the way to Royal Suite. Higher classes unlock fancier shop items and discounts.
    • 🛒Station Shop. Spend your points on Hint Tickets, Spare Hearts and Streak Savers, or repaint the train with new liveries and word tile styles.
