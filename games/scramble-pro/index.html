HOW TO PLAY

  • 👆Tap the scrambled letters in order to spell the word before the timer runs out. Tap a filled slot to undo it, or use CLEAR / SHUFFLE for help.
  • ⚡Solving fast scores more points. Chain correct answers for a growing streak bonus.
  • 🏔️The Climb has 13 zones, from The Street up to Galaxy Tycoon. Each zone is 10 rounds. Beat its boss to climb to the next zone. Your progress is saved.
  • 🚐The Big Lap is a 15-stop road trip around Australia's Big Things, from the Big Swoop in Canberra to the Big Tasmanian Devil. It works just like The Climb.
  • 👹Round 10 of every zone is a BOSS ROUND. The tiles get messed with (spinning, drifting, glitching and more). Run out of time and you retry the zone from its first round.
  • 📅Daily Challenge gives everyone the same 15 words, once a day. You start with no lives and earn one at the boss round.
