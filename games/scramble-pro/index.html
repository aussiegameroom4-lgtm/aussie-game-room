HOW TO PLAY

  • 👆Tap the scrambled letters in order to spell the word before the timer runs out. Tap a filled slot to undo it, or use CLEAR / SHUFFLE for help.
  • ⚡Solving fast scores more points. Chain correct answers for streak and combo bonuses. A ticking clock starts when 10 seconds are left.
  • 🗺️Missions: five journeys with a map of stops to climb. Clear every stop to unlock the next mission. Choose one from MISSIONS on the home screen.
  • 👹The last word of every stop is a BOSS ROUND, where the tiles get messed with. Run out of time and you retry the stop from the start.
  • ⭐Stars: clearing a stop earns 1 to 3 stars. The faster you answer, the more you earn. Replay any stop to improve. Finish a mission to post your total on the global leaderboard.
  • 📅Daily Challenge: everyone gets the same 15 words, once a day. Finish it to build your daily streak, then share your result.
  • ♾️Endless Mode: keep going as long as you can. You earn a life every 10 words, but the timer gets shorter. Daily and Endless scores go on the global leaderboard.
  • 🔥Streak rewards: play the Daily on back-to-back days. At 5, 10 and 30 days (then every 30) you earn a badge and a bonus life, used automatically at your next mission stop.
  • 💾Your progress is saved in this browser. Use BACK UP / RESTORE PROGRESS on the home screen to keep a safe copy.
