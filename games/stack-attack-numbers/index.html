HOW TO PLAY

  • Answer the glowing bottom question to clear it.
  • Every 2 right answers throws junk at your opponent.
  • Junk coming at you lands after a few seconds. Answer fast to block it.
  • 4 in a row charges a special attack. Tap it to fire.
  • Wrong answer = locked out for 2 seconds.
  • If your stack goes over the top, you lose.
  • Points: right answers (more for harder questions and streaks), blocking junk and landing specials. Winning adds bonuses for speed, no mistakes and a tidy stack. Wrong answers cost points.

On a computer: 1–4 to answer · Q / W for specials · P to pause · M to mute
